✨ The Experience

Not just ice cream.
A live show.

Step inside and watch your dessert made from scratch — poured onto a −20°F pan, worked with spatulas, and rolled right in front of you. Every order made fresh. No pre-scooped tubs, no shortcuts.

From signature rolled ice cream to dragon's breath, boba drinks, and fully customizable combinations — this is a dessert experience worth talking about.

Our staff will walk you through every option patiently and enthusiastically, no matter how long it takes to find your perfect flavor.

❄️ How It Works

Made live,
in front of you.

01
Choose your base
Classic cream, chocolate, matcha, strawberry, or our vegan almond milk base. Quality ingredients from the start.
02
Watch it freeze
Your mix is poured onto a −20°F pan and worked with spatulas in real time. Liquid becomes ice cream in seconds.
03
The roll
Thin sheets of frozen cream are rolled into tight cylinders and stood upright in a cup — every one made to order.
04
Top it off
Add sauces, fresh fruit, mochi, boba, pocky, or whipped cream — or let us make you a specialty creation.
05
Worth the photo 📸
Every cup is a work of art. And then you eat it. And then you come back.
